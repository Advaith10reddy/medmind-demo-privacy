MedMind demo privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers a local demonstration using fictional patient records and consenting adult testers. It does not cover a hospital deployment or authorize use of real patient records.

Operator and contact

MedMind is operated for this demonstration by M Advaith Reddy. For privacy questions or requests, email projects@hypestudio.buzz. No hospital is identified as the operator unless it has agreed to that role.

Information used in the demo

For a WhatsApp delivery test, the app stores a tester's name and phone number, message text and replies, language choice, consent and opt-out events, delivery status, and related activity logs. Demo records may also contain fictional procedure and appointment details entered by the operator.

Please do not submit real patient records or sensitive medical details. If you send health-related information in a WhatsApp reply, it may be stored and processed by the demo.

Purpose and service providers

This information is used to demonstrate care-protocol message delivery, consent, reply routing, safety escalation, and audit history. It is not used for advertising or sale.

WhatsApp messages pass through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. When AI features are used, reusable message-library text may be sent to OpenAI for translation drafts, incoming replies may be sent for bounded classification, and clinician-entered planning notes may be sent for care-plan organization. Translation drafts require human approval before use. Operators should use fictional content for all tests.

Storage and requests

The current local prototype stores records in its application database. It does not yet have an automatic retention or deletion schedule. To request access, correction, or deletion of demo information, email projects@hypestudio.buzz. The operator handles these requests manually. A separate reviewed policy and operational controls are needed before a real-patient pilot.

WhatsApp choices

Only consenting test recipients should be contacted. A recipient can reply STOP to opt out of further automated WhatsApp messages. A reply of YES after an invitation records consent in the demo.

Updates

This policy will be updated before cloud deployment or a change in how information is processed. The date at the top will be revised when an updated policy is published.
